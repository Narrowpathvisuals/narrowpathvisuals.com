About Andrew

More than a business.
A calling.

I started Narrow Path Visuals after spending years searching for my purpose. As a Christian, I often felt like I didn't have a clear calling or direction for my life. My wife and I began praying consistently, asking God to reveal where He wanted me to serve and use my gifts.

Not long after, a friend asked me to help film a Christian retreat. Since I already had cameras, microphones, and experience creating content for my YouTube channel, I jumped at the opportunity. While serving at that retreat, several people told me, "This is your calling. You're gifted at this. This is what you're meant to do."

"That experience changed everything. From that moment, Narrow Path Visuals was born."

Today, I have the privilege of helping businesses, gyms, churches, conferences, and organizations grow their presence through powerful visual storytelling. Whether it's creating engaging social media content, producing promotional videos, capturing live events, or livestreaming important moments — my goal is simple: help tell your story in a way that connects with people and leaves a lasting impact.

I genuinely love what I do, and I'd be honored to help bring your vision to life and be part of your business's growth journey.
