📌 HOW TO ADD YOUR VIDEOS (REMOVE THIS NOTE BEFORE PUBLISHING)

1. Upload your video to Vimeo.com (free account works fine).
2. Open your video → click Share → copy the embed code.
3. In this file, find <!-- PORTFOLIO ITEM 1 --> and replace the <div class="video-placeholder"> block with:

<div class="video-embed"><iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/YOUR_ID" allow="autoplay; fullscreen" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

Also update the title, category, and year in the .portfolio-caption below each item. Repeat for all 4 slots. Done!
