"Narrow Path" isn't just a name —
it's the reason this exists.

The name comes from Matthew 7:14. The narrow path is the one worth walking. That's the idea behind everything I shoot — not volume, not shortcuts, but doing the work right and doing it with intention.

It started while filming a Christian retreat. I picked up a camera and realized I was made to tell stories through it. That calling became Narrow Path Visuals — a business built on faith, craft, and a genuine drive to capture moments that matter.

"I don't just show up with a camera. I show up to capture something real."

Over the past few years I've covered Hyrox races from Boston to DC — race-day coverage where every station, every struggle, and every finish-line moment gets captured for the athlete who trained months to be there. That race deserves to be remembered.

Now I'm based in Palm Harbor, FL — right in the heart of the Tampa Bay area — and building something bigger. Florida is a fitness-driven, athlete-packed market, and I'm here for it. Hyrox Miami Beach in March 2027 is a home event for me. No travel fees, no excuses, just great coverage.

Beyond Hyrox, I work with gyms, sports organizations, athletes, and brands that need video content that actually performs — not just pretty footage that sits in a folder.
