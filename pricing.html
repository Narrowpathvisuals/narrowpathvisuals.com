Questions

FAQ

How do I book?
DM @narrowpathvisuals on Instagram with your name, race category (singles or doubles), and your estimated heat time. That's it. No forms, no Calendly.
What if I don't know my heat time yet?
That's fine! Just reach out as early as possible to secure your spot. We can coordinate the exact heat time closer to the event.
How soon will I receive my content?
Within 24 hours of your race. You'll get a shareable link to your video and/or a high-res photo gallery while the race day energy is still fresh.
Is photography available outside of Hyrox?
Photography is exclusively offered as part of Hyrox race day packages. For gym content or brand work, video is our focus.
How many spots are available per event?
Spots are limited per event to ensure every athlete gets quality coverage. Book early to guarantee your spot, especially for Boston which is very soon.
